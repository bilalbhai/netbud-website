These terms need business and legal review before publication or reliance. They are a starting document, not a complete service contract. Specific project and plan terms belong in a separate written agreement.

About the website

This website introduces NetBud’s digital services and allows visitors to prepare callback, project, preview and package enquiries. Service information is general and may be refined when the scope is discussed.

Enquiries and the basket

Adding items to a basket, preparing an enquiry, opening an email draft or opening WhatsApp does not confirm an order, reserve capacity, activate a subscription or establish that payment has been received.

An email draft or WhatsApp message must still be sent by you through the relevant service. If a configured form service reports receipt, this indicates that the delivery service accepted the request; it does not itself mean NetBud has accepted a project.

Pricing and service agreements

Where pricing is shown as “Request a Quote” or “To be confirmed”, NetBud will need to discuss the work before providing a price. Illustrative or planning figures are not approved public offers.

A written proposal or service agreement should confirm scope, deliverables, exclusions, fees, tax treatment, payment schedule, third-party charges, project dependencies and any recurring service terms before work proceeds.

Monthly plans can involve separate setup and recurring charges. Any minimum term, cancellation process, remaining commitment or handover arrangement must be reviewed in the specific agreement.

Free homepage previews

A free preview request is assessed for suitability and availability after discovery. Where offered, it explores one limited homepage concept. It is not a complete working website or a commitment to unlimited design work or revisions.

The target is three business days after discovery, subject to eligibility, agreed information and availability. Timing should be confirmed directly. A full build or further work requires a separately agreed paid scope.

Materials and permissions

Only provide content, images, logos and other materials that you are authorised to share for the project. Avoid sharing private customer records, passwords or sensitive personal information through public enquiry forms.

Ownership, licensing and permitted use of completed work, source files, third-party assets and concepts should be addressed in the project agreement. Do not assume that a preview transfers ownership or grants permission to publish it.

Portfolio and demonstrations

Fictional demonstrations are labelled as concepts and do not represent commercial client results. Named client projects and their materials are published only when authorised. No demonstration implies endorsement by a real business.

Third-party providers

Domains, hosting, email, booking systems, payment providers and other external tools may have their own fees, terms, capabilities and availability. These arrangements should be identified in the relevant proposal.

NetBud cannot confirm an external payment from a screenshot or the act of opening a payment link. Any payment confirmation must come through the applicable provider and agreed business process.

Search visibility and outcomes

Search performance and business outcomes depend on many factors. The website does not promise particular rankings, enquiry volumes, sales or commercial results.

Website use

Use the website for legitimate enquiries and ordinary browsing. Do not submit misleading information, attempt to interfere with its operation or use forms to send unsolicited material.

Privacy and confidential previews

See the privacy draft for the intended handling of website and enquiry information. Public static preview files are accessible to anyone with access to their hosting location; a hidden link or search indexing instruction is not access protection.

Questions and finalisation

For questions, contact NetBud by email. The final terms should identify the responsible legal business, approved effective date and any provisions required for the actual services. Nothing in this draft is intended to remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
