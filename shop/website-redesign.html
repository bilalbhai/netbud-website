We review and refresh one existing page through agreed code and layout changes where its architecture supports them. The focus is clearer calls to action, typography, spacing and mobile improvements. Moving a builder-based site to a custom-coded website is a separate rebuild quote, not part of this one-page refresh.

Why custom-coded?

For a new custom-coded build, these are the benefits we plan for. A one-page redesign works within your existing architecture; a full rebuild or platform migration needs a separate quote.

  • A design shaped around your business instead of a preset template.
  • More control over page layouts and the customer experience.
  • Functionality planned around actual customer needs.
  • Clean, maintainable code that can be extended within the agreed architecture.
  • Appropriate third-party tools for payments, bookings, email and other agreed needs.

Responsive layouts, accessibility and performance are considered during development. Extra pages, custom features and integrations are quoted separately; outcomes depend on implementation.

Good questions. Clear answers.

Will this replace my whole website?

No. The starting scope is one page.

Can every mobile issue be fixed?

Platform and code constraints are reviewed before agreeing feasible work.

Do you use Wix, Shopify or Squarespace?

No. NetBud's website-development service focuses on custom-coded websites built around each client's requirements. We may integrate trusted third-party services for payments, booking, email and other functionality when needed.

Can I request custom website features?

Absolutely. Tell us what your business needs, and we'll assess what's possible and provide a quote before work begins.

Will my website work on mobile?

Our new website projects include responsive layouts designed for mobile, tablet and desktop screens. For a redesign, improvements are agreed after reviewing the existing architecture.

Can I update my website later?

Yes. We can discuss future updates, new pages and additional features. The cost depends on the work involved. Editing tools or a CMS are included only if agreed.

Do I need ongoing hosting?

Yes, a live website requires hosting. We'll explain the available hosting arrangements and ongoing costs before launch.
