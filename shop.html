Ask us a question
Am I paying when I add something to the basket?

No. The basket prepares an enquiry. You review the draft, send it through your chosen app, then agree a written quote with NetBud before proceeding.

What affects the final price?

Scope, quantities, custom artwork, delivery, subscriptions and integrations. Monthly terms and any setup fee are confirmed separately in your proposal.

Does an NFC card work on every phone?

Device compatibility and NFC settings vary. A QR code provides an alternative. We test the supplied destination before dispatch.

Can I send artwork?

Yes. Tell us what you have when enquiring. We’ll arrange a suitable transfer method; this site does not upload files.

Do you charge GST?

NetBud is not registered for GST and does not charge GST. All quoted amounts are Australian dollars.
