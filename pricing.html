A few useful answers

A few things to know.

What is included in the social content package?

The starting monthly package includes ten short-form videos in total, prepared for the agreed Reels and TikTok profiles, using shop photos and information from your website. It also covers captions, agreed publishing and a daily pool of twenty relevant hashtags, used within each platform’s limits. In-store video filming is separate. The photo scope, calendar, revisions and approvals are confirmed in your proposal.

Is advertising spend included in the management fee?

No. Your advertising budget is separate from NetBud’s quoted management fee. We agree the platforms, campaign scope and spend before a campaign is launched.

Can I get a one-off project quote?

Yes. Tell us what you need and we’ll scope the work. You can discuss ongoing care separately.

What’s different about a monthly plan?

A monthly arrangement may combine agreed website or support services. Setup charges, included changes, minimum terms and ongoing fees depend on the written proposal.

Are the proposed managed plans a fixed contract?

Managed website options may include a proposed twelve-month arrangement. No term applies simply because you enquire or add a package to your basket. The actual agreement is provided for review before you proceed.

Can I cancel or change a plan?

Cancellation, notice periods, any remaining commitments and handover arrangements will be set out in the agreement for your chosen service. Ask us to explain anything before accepting.

Do enquiries or basket requests activate a subscription?

No. They help us understand your request. Work, payment and recurring services only proceed after the scope and applicable agreement are confirmed.

Are third-party fees included?

Only where the quotation explicitly includes them. Domains, hosting, software subscriptions, transaction fees, paid assets and printing may be separate costs.
