Who this information is for

This draft covers visitors to the NetBud website and people making callback, project, preview or package enquiries. NetBud’s office is at 67-71 Great Western Highway, Parramatta, NSW 2150. For questions about this draft or your information, contact NetBud by email.

Information you choose to provide

Enquiry forms ask for contact details and business information so NetBud can understand and respond to your request. Depending on the form, this can include your name, business name, phone number, email address, website, service interests, preferred contact time and project notes.

A package enquiry can also include selected items, quantities, instructions and an address where delivery of physical products is needed. Please do not provide passwords, card details, health information or other sensitive personal information through these forms.

How an enquiry is delivered

Enquiries submitted on this website are sent through FormSubmit to bilal@netbud.com.au. The form sends the contact and business details you enter, the type of enquiry and the page it came from. FormSubmit and the receiving email provider process that information to deliver and handle your request. You can instead contact NetBud directly by phone or email.

Where email draft delivery is offered, the website prepares an email for your review. Preparing a draft does not send it. Sending it uses your chosen email application and email provider, whose terms and handling also apply.

If WhatsApp is enabled and you choose that option, selected enquiry details are placed into a prepared WhatsApp message. WhatsApp opens separately, and you decide whether to send it. WhatsApp and its provider apply their own terms and privacy practices.

Why the details are used

The intended purposes are to respond to enquiries, arrange discovery conversations, prepare quotations, discuss requested services and manage an agreed customer relationship. Marketing permission is optional and separate from making an enquiry.

Leaving the marketing box unchecked does not prevent an ordinary response about your request. Any marketing activity, subscriber provider and unsubscribe process must reflect the arrangements actually adopted by NetBud.

Local basket storage

The website attempts to save basket item identifiers and quantities in your browser’s local storage. This helps retain your selections when you return. Your browser settings may block or remove this storage, and behaviour can differ when opening local files.

You can empty the basket on the website or remove site storage through your browser settings. Enquiry form contents are not intended to be stored with the basket.

Fonts, hosting and optional analytics

Google Fonts is requested through an external stylesheet. Your browser makes requests to Google to obtain those font files; information available during those requests can include technical connection details. System fonts are used if those resources are unavailable.

The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection information when serving pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement. This does not mean NetBud has approved or activated a customer ordering backend.

Optional Google Analytics. Analytics is currently disabled pending a configuration review. If re-enabled after that review, Google Analytics 4 through Google Tag Manager would be used only with your consent to understand public page visits, product interest, basket steps and successful enquiries. Google tracking stays off until you accept analytics. You can reject optional analytics or change your choice later using Analytics preferences. Withdrawing consent refreshes the page to stop installed tracking scripts; unsaved entries may be lost.

Analytics events exclude names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, form messages, artwork, order identifiers and verification codes. Private order pages and staff areas do not load Google analytics. Advertising, remarketing and enhanced conversions remain off. Google may process analytics information outside Australia. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy. Essential basket and security storage work independently of analytics consent.

Payments and external services

This static website does not collect credit card details. If a hosted payment link is enabled, payment details are entered on the external provider’s service. The provider’s own terms and privacy information apply.

External websites, booking tools, social networks and other linked services operate under their own policies. Check those policies before sharing information with them.

Access, corrections and retention

You can contact NetBud to ask about the information you have provided or to request a correction. The business must confirm its identification process, response arrangements and any applicable obligations when finalising this policy.

Retention and deletion practices must be established for email, form services, client records and any other configured systems. This draft does not promise a retention period that has not been implemented.

Security and changes

No public website or communication method offers absolute security. Please use enquiry forms only for the information requested, and discuss a suitable channel before sharing confidential project material.

This information may be updated as the site and business arrangements change. The final policy should include its approved effective date and responsible business contact.
