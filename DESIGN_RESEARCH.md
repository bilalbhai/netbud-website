# NetBud redesign research

Research began on 26 October 2026, with a fresh rendered reference pass on 27 October 2026. Twenty current primary agency websites were reviewed through live content/navigation exports and actual browser captures. This was not research from award-directory descriptions or memory. The table distinguishes exported content from what the captures actually show.

All twenty primary URLs were opened again for a later text/navigation refresh. That refresh is separate from the earlier rendered captures: it does not establish that loaders, motion, hover states or mobile layouts were fully inspected.

The fresh pass saved a desktop capture for all twenty references and mobile captures for Ramotion and Studio Freight. KOTA, Huemor, RNO1, Digital Silk, Lounge Lizard, Work & Co, Ramotion and The Working Party supplied useful initial visual evidence. Other captures show partial content, a loader, a blank canvas or an initial brand/navigation shell. Ramotion's mobile capture is useful; the fresh Studio Freight mobile capture is mostly blank apart from its brand and footer. These are captured states, not proof of a complete visual or mobile audit. Motion timing, hidden sections and hover behaviour were not systematically tested.

## A single direction, rather than a collage of references

NetBud uses warm cream and charcoal as the readable base, coral for the main callback action, mango for helpful emphasis, and deep violet/lavender for contrast and supporting illustrations. Space Grotesk headings and DM Sans body text replace the previous uniform Manrope/italic treatment. Original graphic elements show websites, bookings, visibility and ongoing help as one connected service.

The implemented content sequence alternates between a direct offer, compact callback, indexed service choices, a substantial real project, a limited-preview demonstration and practical buying questions. The navigation, pricing and form language stay clear even where the composition becomes more expressive.

The research supplied underlying principles only. No reference logo, illustration, photograph, complete layout, marketing paragraph, result, client list or award was imported. Other agencies' evidence is not evidence about NetBud.

## Limits and retained evidence

The text exports establish current content, exposed controls and links; screenshots establish only what was rendered in those captures. They do not establish every hidden section, transition, form outcome, hover state or responsive state. Several loader/blank captures make those limits material, and they are recorded above instead of being assigned fictional observations.

Detailed source notes are retained locally in `.work/research-agencies-a.md` and `.work/research-agencies-b.md`. Browser visit records and reference/baseline evidence are retained in the task's `netbud-redesign` visualisation folder. The fresh reference evidence includes twenty desktop `reference-*.jpg` captures, `reference-ramotion-mobile.jpg`, `reference-studio-freight-mobile.jpg`, `reference-contact-sheet.jpg` and `reference-rendered-refresh.json`. The contact sheet was visually inspected; the JSON records the actual URLs, titles and initial visible browser states. A saved screenshot of a loader or blank initial canvas is recorded as a limitation, not credited as a complete design inspection.

The implementation's browser checks and three completed review rounds are recorded separately in `REDESIGN_REVIEW.md` and `TESTING.md`. Reference captures and local review crops are evidence files, not deployment dependencies.
